# Experimentation roadmap — SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA context

The simulation illustrates test design; it does not estimate a real treatment effect or justify real budget allocation. Define eligibility, consent, suitability, exposure and verified outcomes before any launch. Randomize at customer level, stratify on pre-treatment variables where useful, retain assignment and analyze intention-to-treat. Avoid simultaneous conflicting treatments. Use an untouched holdout and a pre-registered observation window.

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Control | Treatment | Primary metric | Secondary metrics | Guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payroll | Clear switching guidance improves verified payroll adoption among eligible non-payroll customers | Existing experience / holdout | Payroll explanation plus assisted setup | Incremental verified payroll starts per assigned eligible customer within agreed window | Setup completion, sustained payroll months, product depth | Opt-outs, complaints, support workload, unsuitable targeting |
| Travel service communication | Benefit-focused information improves relevant service activation | Existing generic description | Benefit-specific travel message | Incremental activated eligible customer-service relationships | Active use, retained activation, downstream satisfaction | Misunderstanding, cancellations, complaint rate, support load |
| Push vs Email | Channel timing influences response among customers consented to both | Email | Push, same offer and eligibility | Verified target-action completion per assigned customer | Open/click, cost per incremental completion | Opt-outs, notification fatigue, complaints, unequal delivery |
| Personalization | Explainable context improves suitable product adoption | Generic eligible offer | Transparent pre-treatment context-based message | Incremental active adoption / assigned eligible customers | Depth, service use, satisfaction | Fairness review, inappropriate inference, complaints, cancellations |
| High-value under-engagement | Helpful reactivation restores sustained digital use | No extra message | Service-oriented reactivation support | Incremental 30D active rate after assignment | 90D activity, sessions, satisfaction | Contact fatigue, support burden, complaint/opt-out rates |

Calculate sample size from a verified baseline and a minimum worthwhile effect before launch. The synthetic baseline is unsuitable for production power calculations. Predefine duration, multiple-testing handling and stopping rules; do not repeatedly peek and stop at significance. Report confidence intervals and absolute lift alongside relative lift. Distinguish exposure failures from outcomes and preserve the full assigned denominator.

Use pre-treatment data for segment definitions. Do not use campaign clicks, conversions or post-assignment engagement as predictors of assignment or baseline eligibility. Historical payroll or service activation after a campaign is not automatically attributable to it. If a later ML phase is added, enforce temporal train/test separation, fit preprocessing on training only, prevent future-event leakage, compare calibrated baselines and audit explainability and subgroup performance before real use.
