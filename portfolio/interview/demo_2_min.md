# Two-minute project story

**0:00–0:25 — purpose.** “I started with a simple banking customer snapshot. I wanted to show how fragmented information could become reliable insights about engagement, salary domiciliation and customer opportunities. This is a portfolio demonstration, with clearly labeled synthetic behavior and outcomes.”

**0:25–0:55 — engineering.** “I preserved the source in Bronze, validated and quarantined rows in Silver, and built customer facts and dimensions in Gold. Customer 360 connects the accepted population to product, payroll and digital descriptors. The source reconciles to 1,439 accepted customers and 560 quarantined rows.”

**0:55–1:25 — decisions.** “Power BI makes those views understandable. Four demo models explore targeting, but performance is weak, so I show the baseline and do not claim production effectiveness. Eligibility and contact rules constrain recommendations.”

**1:25–2:00 — evidence.** “I then added journey, retention and simulated experiment analysis. Payroll has an encouraging estimate but misses its planned sample; travel fails a guardrail. That makes the key lesson concrete: a score or a significant result is not enough to scale an action. Automated tests, quality gates and native DAX reconciliation make the local workflow reviewable. The next meaningful step is governed real data and a properly executed experiment.”
