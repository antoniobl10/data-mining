# Business glossary

| Term | Plain-language meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| Primary Bank | A proxy for a deeper everyday banking relationship; not verified customer declaration |
| Payroll / salary domiciliation | Receiving recurring salary through the bank; current state and first adoption are different measures |
| Customer engagement | Meaningful digital activity and relationship use under explicit rules |
| Product penetration | Share of a defined population owning a particular product |
| Product depth | Number of active product types/holdings per customer under the chosen definition |
| Campaign conversion | The predefined outcome within the campaign window; not simply opening a message |
| Propensity | Predicted likelihood of an outcome, not the campaign-caused increase |
| Next Best Action | A proposed eligible action ranked under rules/scores, including a no-action option |
| Retention | Continued or returning activity/ownership under a specific cohort and time definition |
| Churn | Loss of a relationship; the project uses future inactivity as a limited proxy |
| NPS | Respondent-based promoter minus detractor percentage points |
| Customer Value Score | Transparent relative current-value descriptor, not CLV or verified profitability |
| Funnel | Nested populations meeting increasingly restrictive stage criteria |
| Cohort | Customers sharing a defined entry event/period |
| Guardrail | A customer/cost outcome that must remain acceptable even if conversion improves |
| Holdout | A protected comparison population excluded from ordinary campaign contact |
| Incrementality | The outcome difference caused by an intervention under a valid design |
| Attribution | Allocation of observed conversion credit under a rule; not proof of causality |
| SRM | Sample-ratio mismatch: assignment counts inconsistent with the planned allocation |
| Quarantine | Preserved rejected records with reasons, outside accepted analytical outputs |
